Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026 · Last updated 25 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Gopal Sons Jewellers ("we", "us") handles when you use this website, and the choices you have. We have tried to keep it short and plain, because this website is simple: it has no user accounts, no online payments and no shopping cart.

1. Who we are

Gopal Sons Jewellers
Shop No. D-25 & 26, Main Market Marg, Madipur JJ Colony, Paschim Vihar, Delhi 110063, India
Phone / WhatsApp: +91 97113 11111

2. What we collect, and how

  • Visit booking form. If you use "Book a Visit", the name, phone number, area of interest and preferred date you type are used only to build a pre-filled WhatsApp message. Nothing is sent to our servers. We receive your details only if you choose to press send in WhatsApp.
  • WhatsApp and call buttons. Tapping a WhatsApp, call or directions link opens WhatsApp, your phone dialler or Google Maps. What you share from there is handled by those services.
  • Ring designer and collection cards. Your design choices stay in your browser. They are shared with us only if you send them through WhatsApp.
  • Technical data. Like any website, our hosting provider and the third-party services listed below may receive your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, in order to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Your cookie choice. We save your answer to the cookie banner in your browser's local storage so we do not ask again on every visit.

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data, and we do not collect payment or card details through this website.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry or confirm your showroom visit.
  • To keep the website working and secure.
  • To remember your cookie choice.

We process the data you give us on the basis of your consent and, where applicable, for the purpose you provided it for, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other Indian law that applies. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for automated decision-making or advertising profiles.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not set advertising cookies. The only item we store ourselves is your consent choice (local storage key gsj-consent-v1). If you choose "Accept all", we may also load: (a) the Google Maps embed in the Contact section, and (b) anonymous visitor statistics through Google Analytics 4, when we have switched it on, with IP anonymisation, which records pages viewed and taps on our call, WhatsApp and directions buttons. Google may set its own cookies for these. If you choose "Essential only", neither is loaded and a link to open Google Maps is shown instead.

You can change your choice at any time using "Cookie settings" in the footer, and you can clear stored data from your browser settings.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com): delivers the site's typefaces. Your browser contacts Google when the page loads. See Google's privacy policy.
  • jsDelivr CDN (cdn.jsdelivr.net): delivers open-source animation and 3D libraries (GSAP, Lenis, three.js).
  • Google Analytics (only after "Accept all", when enabled): anonymous usage statistics.
  • Google Maps: the embedded map (only after you accept) and the "Get Directions" link.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): used when you choose to message us. See WhatsApp's privacy policy.

These providers act under their own policies. We do not control them.

6. How long we keep data

We do not store form data on this website. Messages you send us on WhatsApp are kept only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry or visit, and for any period the law requires for sales records. Your cookie choice stays in your browser until you clear it.

7. Your rights

Under Indian data protection law you may ask us to tell you what personal data of yours we hold, to correct or complete it, to erase it, or to withdraw your consent. You may also nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will reply within a reasonable time. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India once it is operational for your case.

8. Children

This website is meant for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, tell us and we will delete it.

9. Security

The website is served over HTTPS and contains no database or login. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change is significant, we will say so on the website.

11. Contact and grievances

For any question about this policy or to exercise your rights, contact the Proprietor, Gopal Sons Jewellers, at the address above, or call or WhatsApp +91 97113 11111 during showroom hours (10:30 AM – 9:00 PM, closed on Wednesdays).
